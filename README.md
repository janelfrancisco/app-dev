# My Favorite Series: Wednesday

**Genre:** Comedy, Horror, Mystery  
**Created by:** Alfred Gough and Miles Millar  
**Streaming on:** Netflix  

---

## About the Series
*Wednesday* follows **Wednesday Addams**, a smart and darkly humorous teenager with psychic abilities, as she attends **Nevermore Academy**.  
There, she tries to master her powers, solve a mystery involving her parents, and make sense of new friendships and rivalries.

---

## Why I Love It
- Wednesday’s sarcasm and unique personality  
- The gothic and mysterious setting  
- The detective-style storyline with supernatural twists  
- Jenna Ortega’s amazing performance  

> “I act as if I don’t care if people dislike me. Deep down... I secretly enjoy it.” – Wednesday Addams
